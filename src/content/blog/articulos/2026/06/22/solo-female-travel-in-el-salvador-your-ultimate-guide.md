# Solo Female Travel in El Salvador: Your Ultimate Guide

El Salvador is one of Central America’s best-kept secrets — and solo female travelers who make the trip keep saying the same thing: it completely exceeded their expectations.

Warm locals, world-class surf, volcanic landscapes, colonial towns, and sunsets over the Pacific. Here’s everything you need to know to plan your trip.

---

## Is It Safe?

Yes. In April 2025, the U.S. State Department upgraded El Salvador to a Level 1 travel advisory — the best possible rating, shared with France and Japan. The country has undergone one of the most remarkable safety transformations in the region, and women visiting today consistently report feeling comfortable and welcome.

## Where to Go

**El Zonte**

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/DJI_0797.jpg?resize=749%2C421&ssl=1)

Your home base. A laid-back surf village with world-class waves, beachfront restaurants, and a welcoming mix of locals, expats, and international travelers. Safe, walkable, and genuinely beautiful.

**El Tunco**

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/WhatsApp-Image-2026-06-22-at-1.32.07-PM-edited.jpeg?resize=749%2C467&ssl=1)

10 minutes from El Zonte and a bit livelier. Great surf schools, beach bars, and easy to meet other travelers. Perfect for a day trip or a night out.

**Suchitoto**

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/suchitoto.jpg?resize=749%2C421&ssl=1)

Image by [El Salvador Travel](https://elsalvador.travel/destination/suchitoto/)

A colonial gem about 90 minutes from San Salvador. Cobblestone streets, art galleries, and lake views. A completely different pace — do it as an overnight trip.

**Ruta de las Flores**

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/20250416-DSC08493.jpg?resize=749%2C500&ssl=1)

A scenic highland route connecting charming coffee towns, waterfalls, and local markets. The towns of Ataco and Apaneca are highlights. Best done over two days.

**Santa Ana Volcano**

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/IMG_9931.jpg?resize=749%2C562&ssl=1)

The highest volcano in El Salvador with a stunning emerald crater lake at the top. Book a guided hike and go early — the views are worth every step.

## Getting Around

- Uber is available everywhere and costs $3–8 for most trips. Download it before you arrive.
- Between towns, private shuttles and tour operators are your most comfortable option.
- Along the coast, everything is within walking distance or a short ride away.
- If you prefer to explore at your own pace, car and bike rentals are widely available. They typically start at around $25 per day, making it easy to visit different parts of the country.

## Learn to Surf

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/D169352.jpg?resize=749%2C499&ssl=1)

**Surf City 1** is one of the best places in the world to learn to surf. Beaches like **El Zonte, El Sunzal, and San Blas** offer consistent waves, warm water, and plenty of experienced local instructors. Solo women taking surf lessons is one of the biggest travel trends right now, and for good reason. It’s a great way to meet people, build confidence in the water, and come home with a story worth telling.

## Coffee Shops Are Your Best Friend

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/WhatsApp-Image-2026-06-22-at-2.37.58-PM.jpeg?resize=749%2C766&ssl=1)

Image by [maryinvancity](https://www.instagram.com/p/DJ-ZNsPMue7/?img_index=1)

Solo travel and a good coffee shop go hand in hand — a safe, relaxed spot to work, journal, or just watch the world go by. El Salvador has some of the best coffee in the world, and the café scene reflects it.

- **In San Salvador:** **LUMA** and **Alquimia** are two standout spots. At LUMA, don’t miss their sourdough specialties, which have become a favorite among locals. Alquimia has earned international recognition for the quality of its coffee and its dedication to showcasing Salvadoran beans.
- **On the coast:** Smaller cafés like **Surf Café** in El Tunco and **Cocoa Café** in El Zonte offer a cozy atmosphere, excellent coffee, and refreshing drinks. They’re perfect places to settle in with your laptop, catch up on work, or enjoy a slow morning just steps from the beach.

## Quick Tips

- Download WhatsApp — drivers, restaurants, everything runs on it here.
- The dollar is the currency, and Bitcoin is a tender too, so if you’re carrying BTC you can use it at plenty of spots in around the country.
- Bring reef-safe sunscreen — the Pacific sun is intense, and the reefs will thank you.
- Pack a light scarf — useful for visiting churches, cooler highland evenings, and long bus rides.
- Learn a few words of Spanish — locals love the effort and it goes a long way.
