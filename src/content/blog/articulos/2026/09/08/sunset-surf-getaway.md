# Sunset Surf Getaway

Looking for an oceanfront stay in El Zonte for your next family trip, surf trip, or weekend with friends?

Sunset Surf Getaway is a three-bedroom unit at Barefoot, located right in front of the beach and within walking distance of El Zonte’s point break. With plenty of space, ocean views, and a few extras made for beach days, it’s an easy option for groups who want to stay close to the water.

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/DSC04883.jpg?resize=749%2C500&ssl=1)

#### Three Bedrooms for the Whole Group

The unit has three bedrooms, making it a great fit for families or groups of friends traveling together.

The master bedroom has a king bed and ocean views, while the second bedroom has a queen bed. The third bedroom has a bunk bed, giving the group a little more flexibility depending on who you’re traveling with.

There’s also a comfortable shared living area, kitchen, air conditioning, and high-speed Wi-Fi for everything from longer stays to quick weekend trips.

#### Made for Beach Days

One of the best parts of staying here is how easy it is to spend the day by the ocean.

The unit includes a cooler that you can take down to the beach, as well as mats for yoga, stretching, or simply hanging out on the sand. Grab what you need, walk outside, and you’re already where you came to be.

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/DSC04124.jpg?resize=749%2C500&ssl=1)

#### A Great Base for a Surf Trip

If surfing is the reason for your trip, the location makes things especially convenient.

El Zonte’s point break is within walking distance, so you can check the waves, head out for a session, and be back at the unit without having to organize transportation every time.

And if not everyone in the group surfs, the beach, restaurants, and other spots around El Zonte are also close by.

### Oceanfront in El Zonte

Being right by the beach means the ocean is part of the stay from morning to sunset.

The master bedroom looks out toward the Pacific, and you’re only a few steps away whenever you want to surf, swim, walk the beach, or catch the sunset.
