# How to Spend 6 Days in El Zonte

**Day 1: Arrive, Unpack, Slow Down**

Start by settling into an oceanfront Airbnb. Unpack, enjoy the view, and let the afternoon unfold.

When the sun goes down, head to PROA for dinner. Your first day is about getting into the rhythm of the coast.

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/blog9.png?resize=749%2C500&ssl=1)

**Day 2: Surf & Local Flavors**

Start with a surf lesson from a local instructor, then slow things down over lunch at Nan Tal.

In the evening, keep it local with pupusas from Jeisi. A day can move from the ocean to a great meal without much planning.

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/ChatGPT-Image-2-sept-2026-09_00_56-a.m.png?resize=749%2C500&ssl=1)

**Day 3: Move, Reset, Enjoy**

Take a yoga or functional boxing class at Al Suave Wellness, then leave the afternoon open for a massage.

In the evening, settle in for dinner at La Cajita. After a day of movement and relaxation, there’s no need to fill every hour.

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/ChatGPT-Image-31-ago-2026-12_18_32-p.m.png?resize=749%2C562&ssl=1)

**Day 4: A Slower Morning**  
Begin with a solo yoga session on the deck, with the Pacific in front of you.  

Later, take a Spanish class at Say Zonte and stop for a coffee at Café Cocoa. Experience El Zonte beyond the beach—through movement, language, and local spots.

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/blog5.jpg?resize=749%2C749&ssl=1)

**Day 5: Surf Culture & Dinner**

Take a skate lesson at Puro Surf, followed by lunch at Covana.

Finish the day with dinner at Roka. By now, the days start to feel less like an itinerary and more like your own routine and lifestyle.

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/ChatGPT-Image-31-ago-2026-12_18_35-p.m.png?resize=749%2C562&ssl=1)

**Day 6: Let the Day Flow**  
Spend your final morning at the beach. Take your time. Swim, walk, or simply sit by the water.  
End the trip with a private dinner at your stay.  

Because sometimes the best way to finish a trip is without another place to go.

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/blog8.png?resize=749%2C500&ssl=1)

Six days in El Zonte isn’t about seeing it all.  
It’s about having enough time to enjoy where you are.
