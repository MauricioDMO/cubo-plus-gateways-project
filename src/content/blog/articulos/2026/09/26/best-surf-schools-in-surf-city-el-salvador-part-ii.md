# Best Surf Schools in Surf City, El Salvador — Part II

Surf City is more than a stretch of coastline. It’s a collection of surf towns, each with its own rhythm, waves, and way of life.

In Part II of our guide to the best surf schools in Surf City, we’re heading to El Zonte, El Palmar, El Tunco, and El Sunzal to explore five schools worth knowing before your next surf getaway.

#### ****Bombardos Surf Academy — El Tunco****

Back in El Tunco, **Bombardos Surf Academy** is another option for those looking to spend time in the water.

With several surf spots and a well-established beach culture nearby, El Tunco makes it easy to build a day around surfing and leave room for everything else that comes with being by the coast.

**Location:** El Tunco, El Salvador.

El Tunco is one of the most recognizable stops along the Surf City coastline. Known for its surf culture and lively atmosphere, the town brings together beach days, local food, and evenings that tend to unfold without much planning.

**El Salvador Surf Travel** is a surf school to consider while exploring the area.

#### **Will Surf School — El Zonte**

El Zonte has a way of making you slow down. The atmosphere is laid-back, the coastline is beautiful, and the surf is part of everyday life.

**Will Surf School** is a name to know if you’re planning to spend time in this coastal town.

**Location:** El Zonte, El Salvador.

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/ChatGPT-Image-9-sept-2026-04_19_33-p.m.png?resize=749%2C439&ssl=1)

One of the best parts of exploring Surf City is that no two days have to look the same.

You can spend the morning surfing in El Sunzal, stop for lunch in El Tunco, or take a drive toward El Zonte and discover a quieter side of the coast.

Each town has its own character, and the experience extends well beyond the time you spend in the water.

Because a surf trip isn’t just about the waves. It’s about everything around them.
