# Covana Kitchen: Where to Eat in El Zonte, El Salvador

Located inside Puro Surf Hotel, Covana Kitchen is open to both hotel guests and visitors. It’s a popular breakfast and coffee spot in El Zonte, with a relaxed open-air setting just steps from the beach.

---

## The Setting

Covana Kitchen features an open-air dining area with a large palm-thatched roof and views toward the Pacific. Just steps from the beach, it’s designed to make the most of the ocean breeze and natural surroundings. Mornings tend to be the busiest, while afternoons are generally quieter and more relaxed.

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/SaveClip.App_485148342_1181384607322087_636706843758601943_n-1.jpg?resize=749%2C936&ssl=1)

Image by [covanakitchen](https://www.instagram.com/p/DJwW0AjNHD_/?igsh=bzZxc2c3d2F1c21y)

The design leans into that natural setting rather than fighting it. Wood, thatch, open sightlines to the beach — nothing about the space feels imported or staged.

---

## Breakfast

Breakfast here tends to run long. Carrot pancakes come up often as a favorite among regulars, alongside the usual eggs-and-fruit plates built for people about to go surf or just coming back from it. It’s a solid coffee stop too, one of the better ones in town if you’re working remotely for the morning and want something more substantial than a café pastry.

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/SaveClip.App_494924264_1221731626620718_3758694191898625568_n-rotated.jpg?resize=749%2C599&ssl=1)

## Lunch and Dinner

The lunch and dinner menu combines Salvadoran ingredients with a variety of international dishes. Options include tuna poke bowls, seafood pasta, fish tacos, coconut seafood soup, and ceviche, along with other fresh seafood and seasonal specials. Whether you’re stopping by after the beach or meeting friends for dinner, the menu offers a range of choices for different tastes.

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/SaveClip.App_489075405_1201293695331178_9126318342244215291_n-rotated.jpg?resize=749%2C599&ssl=1)

Image by [covanakitchen](https://www.instagram.com/p/C7E_lXSoJTr/?igsh=MWdhc3czcmE5dTloaA%3D%3D)

The kitchen doesn’t seem interested in choosing a single lane — global techniques and local ingredients.

## Service and Atmosphere

Service is friendly and attentive, and the atmosphere varies throughout the day. Mornings are typically quieter, while lunch and dinner attract a larger crowd of locals and visitors. Weekends are often the busiest, particularly during peak meal times.

## Good for Families and Remote Workers

Covana ends up working for two very different kinds of visitors, often at the same time. Families find it easy, the open layout gives kids room to move, and the menu has enough range that picky and adventurous eaters both find something. For remote workers, the ocean view, decent coffee, and open-air setting make it an easy spot to post up with a laptop for a few hours instead of working from a rental with limited outdoor space.

## Getting There

Covana Kitchen sits inside Puro Surf Hotel at Km 52 on the Carretera del Litoral, right on Playa El Zonte. If you’re staying anywhere along the El Zonte stretch, it’s an easy walk for most rentals in the area — no car needed, and it’s one of the few spots in town where you can eat well without leaving the sound of the waves. For visitors coming from elsewhere along the coast or from San Salvador, it’s a straightforward stop along the Litoral highway, easy to find and well known enough locally that directions are rarely necessary once you’re in town.

## Good to Know

Open daily, roughly 7:30 AM–9:00 PM Sunday through Thursday, and 7:30 AM–10:00 PM Friday and Saturday. Open to the public, not just hotel guests. Reservations aren’t strictly necessary for most meals, but worth considering for dinner on weekends when the crowd picks up. If you’re new to El Zonte, it’s a solid first stop for orienting yourself — a good meal, a view of the water, and a sense of the town’s pace before you head out to explore the rest of the beach.
