# Top Cafés You Must Visit in El Zonte

Coffee is always part of the plan in El Zonte. Between surf sessions and beach days, this town has built up a small but excellent lineup of cafés — from beachfront hideaways to laid-back coffee bars just steps from the sand. Here are five worth adding to your list.

## **Canegue Café**

Tucked under a bamboo and thatch roof, Canegue Café has the kind of low-key, handmade charm that fits right into El Zonte’s pace. The menu is chalked up by hand on a rustic blackboard, surrounded by potted cacti and string lights strung across the wooden beams. It’s open select days of the week, so it’s worth checking before you go — but the open-air, treehouse-like setup makes it a memorable stop between beach walks, especially in the late afternoon light.

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/DSC05118.jpg?resize=749%2C500&ssl=1)

## **Cocoa Café**

A clean, modern space with a full espresso bar, pour-over options, and colorful patterned tile flooring that gives it a distinct look compared to the more rustic, beachy spots in town. The counter setup and proper equipment make Cocoa Café a solid choice if you’re looking for a real specialty coffee experience — pour-overs, espresso drinks, the works — rather than just a quick cup.

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/DSC05131-1.jpg?resize=749%2C500&ssl=1)

## **The Surf Hub**

Part café, part surf culture hangout — The Surf Hub sits right on the beach, with surfboards of every color lining the walls and wooden benches looking straight out at the water. The thatched roof and open sides mean you get the ocean breeze with your coffee, and it’s an easy, casual stop before paddling out or right after a session, sandy feet and all.

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/DSC05095.jpg?resize=749%2C500&ssl=1)

## **Point Break Café**

Set inside a bright, plant-filled courtyard with a pool just steps from the seating area, Point Break Café has a more resort-like feel than most of the other spots on this list. Shaded tables under mature trees and a whitewashed, open-air structure make it a good option if you’re looking for a quieter, more relaxed setting to sit down, work, or just take a break from the sun for a while.

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IMG_5010-1.jpg?resize=749%2C562&ssl=1)

## **Viva Espresso at Covana**

Housed under a striking, hand-built palapa roof with ocean views framed by palm trees, Viva Espresso at Covana blends architecture and atmosphere in a way that makes it worth the stop on looks alone — before the coffee even arrives. Green café chairs and wooden tables sit right at the edge of the space, with the Pacific visible just beyond — a strong pick for golden hour.

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/WhatsApp-Image-2026-08-12-at-6.49.24-PM-1.jpeg?resize=749%2C658&ssl=1)

## **Make It Part of Your Stay**

Whether you’re staying a few days or a few weeks in El Zonte, working your way through these cafés is an easy, low-effort way to explore the town between surf sessions and beach days.
