# Tamanique by ATV: The Waterfalls, Without the Long Hike

A half-day in the hills above El Tunco: dirt roads, open countryside, and cold water at the end of it.

Some of the best days on the coast start by leaving it. Tamanique sits in the hills just above El Tunco, close enough to reach in the morning and back before the light turns gold. Most people know it for its waterfalls. Fewer know that the best way to get there is on four wheels.

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/AI_translates_image_generation_p%E2%80%A6_2K_20261010084246-1.jpg?resize=749%2C418&ssl=1)

#### The ATV Ride

The journey is part of the experience. Riding an ATV through the countryside offers a different perspective on Tamanique, with winding routes, uneven terrain, and views of the surrounding landscape.

It’s an option for travelers who want to spend time outdoors and experience more than the usual sightseeing stops.

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Edit_image_aspect_ratio_2K_20261010084321.jpg?resize=749%2C418&ssl=1)

#### The Tamanique Waterfalls

Reaching the Tamanique waterfalls usually means a long hike down and an even longer one back up. The ATV route skips about 90% of it. From where you park, it’s a 7 to 10 minute walk to the first waterfall.

The setting is rocky and green, with water falling into natural pools. There’s time to walk around, climb a little, cool off, or just sit and listen to the water. The ground can be uneven depending on the weather and trail conditions, so wear proper shoes and follow the guide’s lead.
