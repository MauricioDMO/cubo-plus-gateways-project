# Getaways Guides: El Zonte

Welcome to [**El Zonte**](https://goodlifeelsalvador.com/property-item/zonte/), the vibrant beach town that seamlessly combines the relaxed atmosphere of coastal living with the innovative spirit of Bitcoin Beach. This small community is known for its friendly locals and year-round surfing opportunities. Spend enough time here, and you’ll soon be on a first-name basis with everyone—from the local fishermen to small business owners. Whether you’re visiting to surf, enjoy local cuisine, or explore the unique community, this guide will help you make the most of your stay.

## Morning Bliss: Sunrise, Surf, and Breakfast at Tecos

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/BYFER-4.jpg?resize=749%2C500&ssl=1)

Begin your day early by witnessing a breathtaking sunrise over the ocean. After soaking in the view, grab your surfboard or rent one from local spots like [Tres Hermanos](https://maps.app.goo.gl/RwaRpfqg4ENbTo1M9), [Esencia Nativa](https://maps.app.goo.gl/qd7BvLPrKJBBM7wU7), or [Puro Surf](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Ao8LcuhVkmUi7LBE7), which also offer lessons for beginners. Surf enthusiasts will find waves for every skill level—whether it’s the right-hand point break for advanced surfers or the left-hand beach break. Once you’ve worked up an appetite, head to [Tecos](https://maps.app.goo.gl/nSqjvjkSRG68PMN79), a local favorite in front of El Zonte’s iconic surf point. Enjoy a típico salvadoreño breakfast—a traditional Salvadoran meal that’s both delicious and satisfying—to fuel you for the adventures ahead.

## Where to Eat & Drink

**Lunch at Olas Permanentes**

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/olas-permanentes-el-zonte-1536x1024-1.webp?resize=749%2C500&ssl=1)

Savor the coastal flavors at [Olas Permanentes](https://maps.app.goo.gl/yZRv2dTccyftuahC6), the perfect lunch spot for seafood lovers. Their ceviches are always a favorite, offering an authentic taste of the local cuisine. With the ocean right in front, it’s not just a place to eat—it’s a place to experience. Take a refreshing swim while you wait for your food and enjoy the unique charm of dining where Bitcoin is accepted.

**Dinner at La Cajita Kitchen**

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/strawberry-banana-and.jpg?resize=749%2C416&ssl=1)

For dinner, head to [La Cajita Kitchen](https://x.com/LaCajitaKitchen) — a laid-back spot with beautiful views of the El Zonte river and the waves. Influenced by Chef Ricky of El Encanto and its French owners, the menu blends international flavors with local charm. Enjoy incredible crêpes, salmon pasta, and satisfying burgers, paired with refreshing drinks while watching the sunset. The restaurant also accepts Bitcoin, adding a modern touch to the experience.

**Coffee with an ocean view**

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/BYFER-7.jpg?resize=749%2C500&ssl=1)

Looking for the perfect place to unwind with coffee? Visit [Nan-tal,](https://maps.app.goo.gl/5ur6x6D6FoqvFhBPA) a cozy spot located inside Palo Verde Hotel, offering stunning ocean views. Relax to the sound of waves as you sip on your favorite brew. Don’t miss their special treat: artisanal plantain ice cream, a unique local delight that comes highly recommended! Nan-tal also serves delicious breakfast and dinner, making it a great place to enjoy a full day of relaxing and indulging.

**Jeisi’s Pupuseria**

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/BYFER-3.jpg?resize=749%2C500&ssl=1)

[Jeisi’s Pupuseria](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Kvch2tb1DZphLrRa8) is renowned for serving some of the best pupusas in town. This family-owned eatery offers a wide variety of pupusas, including traditional corn and rice options, as well as unique vegan choices like garlic-flavored pupusas. Despite being busy, the friendly staff and delicious, affordable food make it worth the wait. Prices range from 75 cents to $1.50 per pupusa. Oh, and a quick heads-up: they take a well-deserved break on Wednesdays, so plan your pupusa cravings accordingly!

## Attractions to Visit

**Explore the El Zonte Caves**

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/DJI_0796.jpg?resize=749%2C421&ssl=1)

For adventure seekers, the El Zonte caves are a must-visit and truly a one-of-a-kind hidden gem. To find them, when you are facing the ocean, walk to your right along the beach, all the way to the end. You’ll cross a rock formation, and just beyond it, you’ll discover the caves. Be sure to plan your visit during low tide for safe exploration!

**Skate Park**

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/el-zonte-15-1536x1024-1.webp?resize=749%2C500&ssl=1)

[El Zonte Skate Bowl](https://maps.app.goo.gl/m4vf5doM14UWzdaJ8) is the perfect place for the whole family. This vibrant skate park offers a dynamic space where kids, locals, and the skate community can gather, practice tricks, and enjoy the thrill of the sport. Beyond being a hub for skaters, the space also hosts events organized by the community and local brands, including memorable gatherings by Grlswirl. It’s an energetic hangout spot that brings fun and connection to the area, making it a must-visit for anyone in El Zonte. Plus, you’ll find the public beach entrance here.

**Bitcoin-Friendly Local Shops**

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/BYFER-10.jpg?resize=749%2C500&ssl=1)

El Zonte is leading the way in Bitcoin adoption, as many local shops accept Bitcoin as a payment method. Whether you’re purchasing fresh vegetables, eggs, beer, water, or even a pair of shorts, it provides a seamless opportunity to witness the digital currency revolution in action.

**Learn at the Bitcoin Beach Office**

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/BYFER-17.jpg?resize=749%2C500&ssl=1)

Discover how Bitcoin is empowering communities in El Salvador. Visit the [Bitcoin Beach Office](https://maps.app.goo.gl/6wd5CBL6SpACidxT9), where you can learn about inspiring local initiatives and the broader impact of Bitcoin in the area.

**Visit the Bitcoin Hardware Store**

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/BYFER-2.jpg?resize=749%2C500&ssl=1)

[The Bitcoin Hardware Store](https://maps.app.goo.gl/aGojNFnKZY5BSb5AA) embodies El Zonte’s innovative spirit. This unique establishment offers a variety of products, including hardware wallets, seed metals, textbooks, business terminals, and fun merchandise. They also provide Bitcoin integration and recovery services, making it a valuable resource for newcomers and seasoned Bitcoin enthusiasts. The store also features a Bitcoin Lightning ATM, offering a convenient way to buy or interact with Bitcoin on-site.

## Tips & Advice

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/BYFER-12.jpg?resize=749%2C500&ssl=1)

- **Need to cross the river?** The new bridge in El Zonte, located right on the street in front of [El Vikingo restaurant](https://maps.app.goo.gl/FjgnvYeL21DosQKC9), allows residents and visitors to cross the river with ease. It provides safe and convenient access between different parts of the community, making it easier to explore all that El Zonte has to offer.
- **ATM Location**: For your banking needs, the only [ATM in El Zonte](https://maps.app.goo.gl/eTRNzY36AZn8Gest7) is conveniently located right next to La Canasta Shop. This ensures locals and tourists can easily access cash without venturing far from the heart of town.
- **Local events and meetups:** Look out for local events that might align with your stay. Every Friday night there is a Bitcoin dinner and the last Friday of every month a Bitcoin meetup at Palo Verde hotel. You will also find plenty of other activities and classes like yoga at sunset, salsa classes and more. Simply ask around town and you’re sure to figure out what’s happening.

## Breathtaking Sunsets

![](https://i0.wp.com/getawayselsalvador.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/el-zonte-26-1536x1024-1.webp?resize=749%2C500&ssl=1)

End your day by witnessing one of El Zonte’s breathtaking sunsets—nature’s masterpiece that never fails to inspire awe. The best spots to soak in the magic are at the Bocana of the river or right in front of Olas Permanentes. From October to March, you can even catch the sun setting directly into the ocean, creating a truly unforgettable view. It’s moments like this that lead most visitors to ‘extend their stay’. Enjoy!
