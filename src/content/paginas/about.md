# About Us

Getaways El Salvador is a boutique rental and property management company based in El Zonte, powered by Goodlife Real Estate Consulting.

With more than five years of experience in real estate across El Salvador, our team has developed a deep understanding of the local market and the needs of both property owners and travelers. Expanding from real estate consulting into rental and property management was a natural next step.

We provide a full-service approach, helping homeowners maximize their investment while ensuring guests enjoy a seamless and memorable stay on El Salvador’s beautiful Pacific coast.

Built on real estate expertise and strong local roots, Getaways El Salvador brings a personalized, hands-on approach to every property and every guest.

**Welcome to Getaways El Salvador.**
